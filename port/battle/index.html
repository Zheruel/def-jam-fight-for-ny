Controls

Player 1 moves with WASD. J / U / I / O press Triangle / Square / Cross / Circle. Q / E and R / F control the shoulder buttons. T / V and Z / C move the right stick up / down and left / right.

Player 2 moves with the arrows. L / K / [ / ] press Triangle / Square / Cross / Circle. Comma / period and slash / semicolon control the shoulder buttons. Numpad 8 / 2 and 4 / 6 move the right stick. Both sticks work on standard gamepads.

When the Blazin’ indicator appears, tap the right stick to activate it. Grapple with Cross, then tap the right stick again for the signature move.
